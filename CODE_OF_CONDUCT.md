# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

USBUtils exists so PlayStation 2 fans can manage their game drives without
friction. We want the community around it to feel the same way: a place
where anyone can ask, learn, and build, no matter their experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
- Assume good faith. Most problems are misunderstandings, not attacks.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and, when you disagree, explain why.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully. It is about the code, not you.
- When you are unsure whether something is okay to post, ask first.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discrimination based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
  identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
  religion, sexual identity, or orientation
- Publishing other people's private information, such as physical or email
  addresses, without their permission
- Trolling, baiting, or any deliberately disruptive behavior
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Anything you would not say to a person face to face

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, pull requests, and other contributions that break this code of
conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please let the
maintainer know so it can be addressed:

- Open an issue at
  [github.com/AldySan/USBUtils_src/issues](https://github.com/AldySan/USBUtils_src/issues)
  and @mention **@AldySan**, or
- for sensitive matters, reach out privately through Ko-fi:
  [ko-fi.com/aldysan](https://ko-fi.com/aldysan)

All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion. Reports made in good
faith will never be held against the person who made them.
